How it fits

The gap: the work between your inbox and your billing system

Move-in and Move-out

AI reads the form in whatever shape it arrives, checks the address against the grid register, opens or closes the contract, and asks the customer for the one field that is missing.

Meter Readings

AI takes readings from photos, emails and the portal, tests them against the consumption history, books the plausible ones and routes the rest to a clerk.

Billing Corrections

AI finds the invoice a complaint refers to, recalculates it against the tariff, and prepares the correction for approval.

Supplier Switches

AI answers market messages inside the deadline and flags the ones where the master data disagrees.

Before you decide

What people ask first

How accurate is it in the first week?

We calibrate on a sample of your own requests before go-live. Readings typically start at about 85% booked without a person and reach the mid-nineties within weeks. Below your threshold, a clerk decides.

What does a pilot involve?

One process, 30 to 50 real cases and the matching master data. It runs in a separate environment, needs no integration, and costs nothing.

Where does our data live?

In an environment of its own, encrypted, hosted in the EU. Every change is logged, and nothing you send is used to train models for anyone else.
