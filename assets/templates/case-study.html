About Stadtwerke Lahn

Stadtwerke Lahn supplies electricity, gas and water to around 64,000 meters across four districts. Its customer service team of fourteen handles every contract change, reading and billing question, most of them arriving by email or post.

Use-case definition

The focus is annual meter reading intake: readings that arrive as photos, handwritten cards, emails and portal entries, often late and sometimes contradicting each other.

The challenge

Every October, one team keys tens of thousands of readings by hand. Volumes rise each year while the team does not.

Three things make it worse each year:

Three screens per reading

Each reading means the mailbox, a spreadsheet and the billing system, typed across by hand.

A six-week peak

Six weeks carry most of the year’s volume, and temporary staff need training each time.

More photos, fewer cards

More customers send photos instead of cards, which take longer to read and check.

So the process is slowest in the six weeks that matter, and it depends on who is in the office.

The solution

Northdesk works on top of the billing system already in place. Nothing was migrated. It reads each reading, checks it against the consumption history, and books the ones it is sure about.

Where a clerk still decides:

AI readsEvery photo, card and email
→
Clerk decidesOnly the uncertain ones
→
System booksThe rest, with a log entry each
Clerks keep the judgment calls. The keying is gone.
